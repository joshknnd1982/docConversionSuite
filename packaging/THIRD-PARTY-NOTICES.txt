NVIDIA CUDA engine (optional download, not in the installer)
-----------------------------------------------------------
When you choose "NVIDIA GPU with CUDA" and press Download Vision Model, the
CUDA build of llama.cpp b12441 (MIT) and the CUDA runtime libraries it
needs (cudart, cuBLAS), as packaged in the official llama.cpp release, are
downloaded from https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/releases/tag/b12441
into %LOCALAPPDATA%\DocConversionSuite\vision. The CUDA runtime libraries
are NVIDIA software distributed under the NVIDIA CUDA Toolkit End User
License Agreement (https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/eula/).
